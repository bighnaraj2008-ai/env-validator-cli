# Validation Log Archiving and Retention Policy

This policy governs the retention, archiving, and rotation of validation logs
and telemetry generated by `env-validator-cli`.

---

## 1. Retention Schedules

| Environment | Retention Window | Storage Tier | Pruning Utility |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| Local Development | 3 days | Local disk | `scripts/prune_logs.py --retention-days 3` |
| CI Test Runners | 7 days | Ephemeral runner | `scripts/prune_logs.py --retention-days 7` |
| Production Release | 90 days | Cold archive | `scripts/export_validation_logs.py` |

---

## 2. Automated Pruning Protocol

1. Run `python scripts/prune_logs.py --logs-dir logs/ --retention-days 7 --dry-run` to inspect files.
2. Execute with `--manifest prune_manifest.json` to generate an audit trail.
